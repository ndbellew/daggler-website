## Objective

Explain a narrow defensive task that needed to become safer, faster, or more repeatable. State who operated the workflow and what actions could have caused harm.

## Design

Document input validation, dry-run behavior, least privilege, secrets handling, audit logs, and failure recovery. Make dangerous operations explicit and require review at the right boundary.

## Lessons

Describe what the automation did not attempt to solve. For operational tools, predictable behavior and a clear escape hatch often matter more than feature count.
