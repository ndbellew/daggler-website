> **Sample content:** This is a writing and layout example. It does not describe a real system, employer, result, or personal contribution.

## Context

Use this section to explain the environment and the problem in language that a technical reader can verify. Include scale only when you have defensible numbers. Identify the users, operating constraints, and why the existing approach was insufficient.

## Constraints

- State the reliability or availability objective.
- Name security, compliance, staffing, or migration boundaries.
- Separate requirements from assumptions.
- Avoid revealing confidential architecture or customer information.

## Approach

Describe the architecture at the level needed to understand your decisions. A strong case study explains alternatives considered, why one was selected, and how risk was reduced during rollout.

```text
request → policy boundary → service → durable dependency
                    ↘ telemetry → alert → response
```

## Verification

Explain testing, observability, deployment controls, rollback behavior, and how the team knew the work improved the system. Replace generic claims with measurements only when they are real and attributable.

## Retrospective

Close with what changed, what remained unresolved, and what you would do differently. Honest boundaries are more useful than a perfect narrative.
