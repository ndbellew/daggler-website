Automation earns trust when people can predict what it will do. Speed matters, but legibility, recoverability, and safe defaults matter first.

## Show intent before mutation

For consequential changes, render a precise preview: targets, old and new values, skipped items, and policy checks. A preview should come from the same plan that execution uses.

## Make retries safe

Idempotency is not just an API property. Scripts and workflows need stable identifiers, checkpoints, and behavior for partially completed runs.

## Leave useful evidence

Good automation reports what happened in terms an operator can act on. Logs should preserve correlation IDs, policy decisions, timing, and clear remediation without exposing secrets.
