## Summary

This document proposes the Bellew-Norrie Method as a software-design approach for small teams and companies developing new products. It combines Object-Oriented Software Engineering, Domain-Driven Design, Yourdon–Constantine structured design, and Design by Contract around six qualities: traceability, readability, cohesion, low coupling, explicit contracts, and invariant protection.

Using a grocery store’s online ordering system as a recurring example, the document explains how use cases, shared business terminology, focused components, controlled interfaces, and enforced domain rules can support future changes. It advocates concentrating design effort on core components while keeping temporary supporting utilities lightweight without duplicating or bypassing core rules.

The document presents a conceptual method supported by cited literature and illustrative examples. It does not report an empirical evaluation of the method.
